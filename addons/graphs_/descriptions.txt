# Descriptions of this pack's modules, shown when the ? on a module is clicked.
# Each module starts with its name in square brackets, exactly as it is shown on the toolbar.
# A blank line starts a new paragraph. Lines starting with # are notes and aren't shown.

[Pie Chart]
Shows each category's share of the whole. Choose a category column (Cat), and optionally a numeric column to add up; without one, each category's number of rows is used.

The biggest slices are drawn on their own and the rest are joined into one Other slice. The output lists each category's total and share, largest first.

[Histogram]
Shows how a numeric variable is distributed by counting its values in bins. The number of bins is chosen automatically, or can be stepped up and down, and a fitted normal curve can be drawn over the bars.

A short note says whether the data looks normally distributed. The output gives the start, end, count and share of each bin.

[Box Plot]
Shows the spread of a numeric variable (Y), optionally split into groups (By). Each box covers the middle half of the values with a line at the median; the whiskers reach the furthest values within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and points beyond them are outliers.

The output gives each group's count, five-number summary, mean and number of outliers.
